# Pilot Limitations

- Mock smoke only; no live provider evidence.
- No human reviewers; blind pack generated but unrated.
- Scores are protocol artifacts, not quality claims.
- The three mock pipelines do not have equal tools, evidence, or budgets and
  cannot support a fair comparison.
- Single and legacy manifests prove zero provider attempts; token totals are unavailable.
- The reviewer pack withholds its shuffle seed; unblinding must remain separate.
- Dataset covers 5 cases from one small fixture repository.
